Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Doksy (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS document vault app and related services.

Privacy-first by design: Your scanned documents, photos, PDFs, and family profile data are stored locally on your iPhone. We do not operate cloud servers for your vault content, and we do not require you to create an account to use Doksy.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us:

  • Email: info@hexalitics.com
  • Website: hexalitics.com

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to operate Doksy and improve the app. Types of information include:

  • Information you store in the app (on your device): family profile names, relationships, optional notes and dates of birth, document titles, tags, categories (Local or Private), scanned images, imported photos, PDF pages, and optional document notes. This data is saved on your iPhone using on-device storage and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • App preferences: settings such as your display name, selected profile, custom document tags, and whether Face ID / Touch ID app lock is enabled. These are stored locally on your device.
  • Subscription and purchase data: if you subscribe to Doksy Pro, Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status (e.g. whether Pro is active). RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information from Apple — not your vault documents.
  • Feedback you choose to send: if you use the in-app feedback form (powered by FeedJar), we receive the message and any details you include, such as your email if you provide it.
  • Analytics and crash data: we may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage patterns and diagnose crashes. This may include device model, operating system version, app version, and anonymized usage events. It does not include the contents of your documents.
  • Biometric authentication: if you enable app lock, Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode is handled entirely by iOS. We do not collect or store your biometric data.

We do not collect HealthKit data, location data, contacts, or advertising identifiers for targeted advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Doksy on iOS.
  • Enable core features: document scanning, import, organization, tagging, PDF export, family profiles, and optional app lock.
  • Verify and manage Doksy Pro subscriptions.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback you submit.
  • Analyze anonymized usage and crash reports to improve stability and features.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical or security issues.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. On-Device Storage & Your Control

Your vault content remains on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share it (for example, exporting a password-protected PDF or a JSON metadata backup). You can delete individual documents, family profiles, or all app data at any time from within Doksy.

If you uninstall the app, locally stored vault data is removed from your device subject to iOS system behavior and any device backups you maintain.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (If applicable)

Where applicable (for users in the EU/EEA or UK), we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract to provide the services you request.
  • Legitimate interests such as improving Doksy, securing our systems, and preventing fraud.
  • Your consent for optional features (e.g. submitting feedback or analytics where consent is required).

For users in India, we process personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable rules.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not upload your documents to our servers. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: vendors who perform services on our behalf, including RevenueCat (subscriptions), FeedJar (feedback), Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting), and Apple (App Store payments). They process data only as needed to provide their services and are contractually required to protect it.
  • When you share content: if you export a PDF, share a backup file, or use the iOS share sheet, you control where that data goes.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.
  • Business transfers: if Hexalitics or Doksy is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, limited operational data (not your on-device vault contents) may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • Aggregate and anonymized data: we may use de-identified data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you (e.g. overall crash rates or feature usage trends).

6. Sensitive Data & Identity Documents

Doksy is designed to store sensitive identity documents such as passports, national IDs, visas, and certificates. This information is treated as sensitive personal data.

  • Vault content is stored only on your device and is protected by iOS security and optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock.
  • We do not access, view, or mine the contents of your documents on our servers because those contents are not transmitted to us.
  • You are responsible for safeguarding your device, passcode, and any passwords you set on exported PDFs.

7. Cookies & Tracking

The Doksy iOS app does not use browser cookies. Third-party SDKs (such as Firebase) may use device identifiers or similar technologies for analytics and crash reporting as described above. You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings; Doksy does not serve third-party ads.

8. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect data we process. On-device vault data is protected by iOS sandboxing and optional biometric app lock. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data you choose to export or share outside the app.

9. Data Retention

  • On-device vault data: retained on your iPhone until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Subscription records: retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their respective policies.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained for a limited period according to Firebase and our internal retention practices.
  • Feedback: retained as long as needed to respond and improve the product, then deleted or anonymized.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, and objection to certain processing.

  • Vault data: you can view, edit, export, and delete your documents and profiles directly in the app.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel Doksy Pro through your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Other requests: contact us at info@hexalitics.com.

Indian users may exercise rights under the DPDP Act, including grievance redressal, by contacting us at the email above.

11. Children

Doksy is a family document organizer and may be used to store documents for family members, including children, by the account holder of the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. If you believe a child has provided us personal information without consent, please contact us.

Doksy may link to third-party sites (for example, our website, App Store pages, or legal documents). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Limited operational data processed by our service providers (such as analytics or subscription management) may be transferred to and processed in India, the United States, or other countries where those providers operate. We take steps designed to ensure an adequate level of protection for such transfers where required by law.
